# PHASE 1 audit — BUY/SELL setup evaluation

Scope: trace every price the trade-analysis panel can display back to its source
(candles vs. fixed offset vs. structure vs. AI), identify root causes of any
unjustified levels, and record what was corrected. The authoritative engine is
`app/analysis/strategy.py::evaluate_trading_setup`; the API surface is
`app/main.py::_evaluate_setup_structured` exposed at `GET/POST /api/v1/analysis/evaluate`.

## Data-flow map (verified)

| Displayed value | Producer | Source of truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current price | `evaluate_trading_setup` | last completed candle close, or live tick when `data_status == "LIVE"` |
| Entry price / zone | `evaluate_trading_setup` §6 | confirmed structure break level (RETEST) or `max(price, EMA20)` / `min(price, EMA20)` (PULLBACK) |
| Stop-loss | `evaluate_trading_setup` §6 | structure break ± `1.5 × ATR14`, or `EMA50 ± 0.2 × ATR` vs `entry ∓ 1.5 × ATR` |
| Take-profit 1/2/3 | `evaluate_trading_setup` §7 via `_plan_take_profits` | R-multiple band (1.5R/2.0R/3.0R) snapped to the nearest real structural level in each band |
| Risk/reward (overall + per target) | `evaluate_trading_setup` §7 | `abs(target − entry) / abs(entry − stop)` |
| Indicators | `analysis/indicators.py` | candle series only |
| AI review text | `main.py` AI review call | **text only; never sets or edits a level** |

No displayed level is hardcoded, and no level is generated by the AI. The frontend
(`App.tsx::reviewSetup`, `components/PriceChart.tsx`) reads entry/stop/targets
straight from the backend evaluation object.

## Root causes found and corrections

| # | Root cause | Evidence in original engine | Correction | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Targets were a single fixed R multiple with no per-target risk/reward and no anchoring to real structure. | §7 computed one `tp1`/`tp2` pair from a fixed distance and a single `rr`. | New `_plan_take_profits` builds a 3-rung ladder: each rung is anchored to its R band and snaps to the nearest real structural level (swing, opposing order block, S/R cluster) inside that band; otherwise the R baseline is used. Per-target `risk_reward_tp1/2/3` added. | `tests/test_strategy.py` TP-ladder tests |
| 2 | A candidate could carry levels while confirmation was still incomplete, and states collapsed to `NO_SETUP`/`WATCHLIST`. | No explicit state machine; `status` alone. | Added `setup_state` ∈ {NO_SETUP, WATCHLIST, WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION, POTENTIAL_BUY, POTENTIAL_SELL, INVALIDATED}. `WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` keeps the deterministic zone but `direction == "NO_SETUP"`; `WATCHLIST` clears levels. | `test_waiting_for_confirmation_*`, `test_min_rr_filter_*` |
| 3 | The engine trusted upstream OHLC without an in-engine integrity check. | Candle validation lived only in the market-data normalizer. | Added `validate_candles`: fatal problems (non-finite / high<low / inconsistent) → `DATA_UNAVAILABLE`; duplicates/misordered timestamps → warnings only. | `test_candle_validation_*`, `test_out_of_order_timestamps_*` |
| 4 | No guard against acting on a setup whose stop was already broken, or on a stale entry after price ran away. | None. | Added `already_invalidated` (latest close beyond the structural stop → `INVALIDATED`, levels cleared) and `late_entry` / `late_entry_reason` (price beyond the entry zone by `max(3 ticks, 0.25 × ATR)`). | `test_invalidated_state_*` |
| 5 | The AI analysis context embedded the full setup objects, unbounded, and could be mistaken for a source of levels. | `main.py` AI context attached raw `buy_setup`/`sell_setup`. | Added `_SETUP_FIELDS` projection so the AI context carries only a bounded numeric/explanation subset; the assistant prompt still forbids inventing or editing levels. | `test_api.py::test_ai_context_is_bounded_and_de_duplicated` |
| 6 | The panel did not show TP3, per-target R:R, stop basis, or data-quality warnings, and unknown statuses fell back to a neutral label. | `App.tsx::SetupResult` rendered only TP1/TP2 and the overall R:R. | Panel now renders TP3, per-target R:R, stop basis, a warnings strip, a late-entry note, and distinct `WAITING FOR CONFIRMATION` / `INVALIDATED` labels; chart draws the TP3 line. | `App.test.tsx` TP3/R:R/warnings and waiting-state tests |

## Rule semantics after correction

- **Entry** is never a market order: `RETEST_ENTRY` waits for a retest of the confirmed
  structure level; `PULLBACK_ENTRY` requires a candle close on the correct side of EMA20.
- **Stop** always sits beyond the structural invalidation with an explicit ATR/tick basis
  (`stop_basis`), and is validated (`BUY: stop < entry`; `SELL: stop > entry`).
- **Targets** are monotonic in the trade direction and each is labelled with its own R:R;
  a target is rejected by the `min_rr` gate when the geometry cannot support it.
- **States** are mutually exclusive and never present a watch/wait state as a confirmed signal.

## Remaining limitations (not silently hidden)

- The structural ladder is band-based; documentation states the band rule. Levels are
  deterministic but have not been calibrated against an external historical dataset.
- High timeframes (4h/1d/1w) may be unavailable through the configured provider quota;
  when unavailable the engine returns the honest data-status path rather than inventing data.
- AI review is qualitative only. If the AI is unavailable, the panel still renders the
  deterministic levels.
